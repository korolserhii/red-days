Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

Who we are

Red Days is an app by Korol Serhii, an independent developer. Contact: korolserhii.dev@gmail.com.

What the app stores

Only what you enter yourself: period dates and flow, symptoms, mood, energy, sleep, notes, life circumstances (for example hormonal contraception, breastfeeding or perimenopause), medications, the date of a possible contact if you use the pregnancy-test helper, and app settings. This is sensitive health information.

Where it is stored

All data stays on your phone in a local database. The app has no account, no server, no ads and no analytics. We do not collect, receive, sell or share your data — we never see it. Android cloud backup is turned off for this app.

Files you create

If you export a backup or a PDF report, the app creates a file and opens the standard Android share sheet. Where that file goes (for example your doctor, email or cloud storage) is your choice, and the privacy policy of that service applies.

Permissions

Notifications — only for optional reminders you turn on. Reminder texts are neutral and do not mention periods.

Deleting your data

Settings → Delete all data removes everything at once. Uninstalling the app also deletes all its data.

Health disclaimer

Red Days is not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, cure or prevent any disease and it is not a method of contraception. The app is intended for adults (18+).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.

Якщо ви експортуєте резервну копію або PDF-звіт, застосунок створює файл і відкриває стандартне меню «Поділитися». Куди надіслати файл (лікарю, поштою, у хмару), вирішуєте ви; далі діють правила обраного сервісу.

Видалення даних

Налаштування → «Видалити всі дані» стирає все одразу. Видалення застосунку також видаляє всі його дані.
